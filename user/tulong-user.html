Humingi ng Tulong

Request assistance from your barangay's SK council — educational, medical, financial, or livelihood support. Fill out the form below and a council member will follow up on your request.

Assistance Request Form

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. Your information is only shared with your barangay's SK council.

Include what the assistance is for and when you need it by.

After submitting, your request goes to your barangay's SK Committee for review. You'll be contacted using the number above to confirm requirements and next steps.

What happens next

  • The SK Committee reviews your request, usually within 3–5 working days.
  • A council member calls or texts you to confirm details and any documents needed.
  • Approved requests are scheduled for release or coordinated with the relevant program.

Bring a valid ID and, if applicable, proof of enrollment or a medical certificate when your request is approved — this speeds up processing.
