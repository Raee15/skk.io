Humingi ng Tulong

Request assistance from your barangay's SK council — educational, medical, financial, or livelihood support.

Please Log In First

To submit an assistance request form, you need an active SK Portal account. Logging in allows us to securely process your request and keep you updated on its status.

Don't have an account yet? Register here

What happens next

  • Log in or register for an account on the SK Portal.
  • Fill out the Tulong Assistance Form with your details and specific requirements.
  • The SK Committee reviews your request, usually within 3–5 working days.
  • A council member contacts you to confirm details and provide updates.

Tip: Keep a valid ID and proof of residence ready when submitting your request to ensure faster processing.
